Mapped from the spec, 1:1 unless noted: ring geometry, drag/throttle behaviour, direct-DOM update during drag, off-state graying, watermark + ambient as non-layout absolute layers, and the hass-update-skip pattern all carry over as written — they're layout/behaviour decisions, not styling.

What changed for Sumi House: tokens now read the same custom-property names HA themes expose (--primary-color, --card-background-color, --divider-color…) mapped to our palette, so the real card inherits themes/sumi-house.yaml automatically rather than hardcoding hex — the spec's own §8 recommendation, applied. Card surface is our cast-concrete texture, not a flat gradient. Fonts are Zen Old Mincho (name), Zen Kaku Gothic New (labels/UI), IBM Plex Mono (every number). Border-radius 10px instead of the sharper Apple-Home corner. No orange pulse border — the "heating" signal is a slow, quiet copper glow at the edge, consistent with every other Sumi House widget: activity shows as a trace of warmth, never a flashing alert. The LED swatch row leads with the house's own copper and oak before the full RGB spectrum, since those are genuinely good sauna-light colors and it ties the widget back to the material palette without limiting the feature.

Left untouched on purpose: the full-spectrum rainbow ambient/effect option — it's a real feature (LED color cycling), not a decorative motif, so it stays outside the restrained palette rather than being tamed to match it.
