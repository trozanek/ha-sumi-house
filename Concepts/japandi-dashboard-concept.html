Home Assistant · Design Concept 墨の家

Sumi House

The system is the house in section: a charred shou sugi ban ground, cards cast in concrete — grain, mottle and all — washi-paper text, oak mid-tones. Copper is the kintsugi — but held to a single gesture: one repaired crack where the charred facade meets the interior, at the head of every view. Everywhere else copper stays liquid: a glaze on active toggles, a current along the energy line, a warm trace on a running card. Quiet by default; gilded where it's awake.

How this maps to Home Assistant. Everything above respects HA's card-grid reality: the layout is a standard sections view (header + grid sections), the room and wellness tiles are custom:button-card / Mushroom templates, dimmers are tile cards with the slider feature, and the palette, 6 px radius and fonts ship as a themes/ YAML plus one card-mod style for the plank header and copper heat-line. Nothing here needs anything more exotic than HACS staples (button-card, card-mod, Mushroom).

Design rules to keep us honest as we build every view: the material hierarchy is the house in section — charred wood is chrome (headers, ground), concrete is surface (every card carries its grain), the kintsugi crack appears exactly once per view — the header seam — and nowhere else; on cards, activity shows as copper glaze and traces, never as cracks. Moss means healthy/idle-ok; mizu is reserved for water systems; text does the work, icons stay optional; and numbers are always mono — the house reports like an instrument, not an app. The crack seams themselves are lightweight inline SVGs, so in HA they drop in as card-mod backgrounds.
